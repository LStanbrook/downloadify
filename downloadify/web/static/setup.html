Set up Spotify login

Regular public playlists need none of this. Do this only if you want Downloadify to reach your private playlists, personalized ones like Discover Weekly, or playlists with more than 100 tracks.

You'll create your own free Spotify app to get a "Client ID", then paste it into Downloadify once. It's free, it doesn't need Spotify Premium, and the ID stays on your computer.

Downloadify only ever reads your playlists with this. It can't post, follow, change anything, or see anything else on your account.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard

    Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with any Spotify account — a free account is fine.

  2. Click "Create app"

    It's a button near the top of the dashboard.

  3. Fill in the form

    • App name: anything — e.g. Downloadify
    • App description: anything — e.g. Personal playlist downloader
    • Redirect URI: type exactly http://127.0.0.1:8080 and click Add
    • Tick the Web API checkbox
    • Agree to the terms, then click Save

  4. Open your new app's Settings

    There's a Settings button on the app's page.

  5. Copy the Client ID

    It's a 32-character string of letters and numbers. Copy that one. You do not need the client secret.

  6. Paste it into Downloadify

    In the app, click Set up Spotify in the top-right, paste the Client ID, and click Save.

  7. Log in

    Click Log in with Spotify and finish signing in in the browser tab that opens. Downloadify remembers it — you only do this once.

Good to know

  • The key is per-person: everyone who wants login creates their own. There's no shared account and no waiting list.
  • If your playlist is 100 tracks or fewer and public, you can skip all of this — it works with no setup.
  • To remove it later, click Change API key in the app and clear the field, or delete .downloadify_settings.json next to the app.
